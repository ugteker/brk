# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People, especially younger learners, who want to understand long-form captioned material without consuming it in full. They add sources such as YouTube podcasts, episodes, and lectures, then use purpose-built agents to extract the context that matters to them. The product must be appealing and usable on both desktop and mobile web.

## Product Purpose

Maydoz turns spoken and written source material into structured, personalized analysis. Users create or refine AI agents with a conversational curator, attach those agents to sources, and receive reports that surface the essence, context, and perspectives relevant to each agent's purpose.

The curator turns a freeform conversation into an editable agent profile that users explicitly confirm before creation; manual setup remains available. Guided setup begins with a source. The selected source is advisory context for the curator, not binding configuration. Choosing **Set up later** saves only the source; it does not create an agent, playbook, or run.

The initial launch focus is education: helping learners gain a comprehensive understanding of a long lecture or podcast without listening to every minute.

## Positioning

Maydoz is individualized, transcript-first learning and research: users choose their own sources and custom agents rather than receiving a generic summary. Its synthetic discussions let agents examine a topic from different perspectives, creating new ways to approach and think about material.

## Operating Context

Users add captioned content, including YouTube videos and podcasts, then connect reusable agents to channels or episodes through playbooks. The platform extracts the spoken text, analyzes it on a schedule, and delivers structured reports in a personal feed and notifications. Finance is an optional specialist character, never the general product path.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Reusable Sources, Agents, and Playbooks let one agent analyze many sources and one source feed many agents.
- Agents have distinct, editable characters such as summarizer, teacher, trainer, philosopher, influencer, and finance expert.
- Reports combine shared insights with character-specific output. Synthetic discussions materialize multiple agent perspectives.
- Non-finance characters must not offer investment advice, tickers, or long/short calls unless the evidence itself is explicitly about finance.
- Agent avatars and advanced runtime configuration are outside the current guided setup scope.

## Brand Commitments

Maydoz should feel precise, intelligent, ambient, sharp, focused, and trustworthy. It prioritizes signal over noise, progressive disclosure, and persona-aware reports. Generic finance, crypto, and cluttered metric-dashboard aesthetics do not represent the product.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains product documentation and runnable web/API applications. No separate customer testimonials, case studies, press, benchmark claims, or brand assets are confirmed; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Extract context, not merely summaries.
2. Let people define the lens through their own agents.
3. Make long-form learning more accessible without flattening nuance.
4. Show multiple perspectives where they improve understanding.
5. Keep finance-specific behavior isolated to finance-specific agents and evidence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Dark mode, reduced-motion support, and adequate tablet touch targets are required. Desktop and mobile web both serve the core audience.
